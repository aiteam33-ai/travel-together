La Nostra Storia

Our story

Chef Marco Bellini

Born in a small village outside Siena, Marco learned to cook at his grandmother's stove, rolling pasta by hand before he could reach the counter without a stool. After years in the kitchens of Florence and Rome, he opened Trattoria del Sole to bring that same unhurried, sun-warmed cooking here — made fresh every day, shared at a table with people who matter.
